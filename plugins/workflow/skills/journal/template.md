## Problem and preceding iteration

Describe what happened, the expected behavior and the impact, using a sanitized example. Link the earlier entry or implementation this builds on. Explain what that evidence established and which new observation changed the next step. Don't imply sole causation from chronology alone.

## Evidence

Label each claim as reported, tested, measured, hypothesis or deferred. Link source records, commits and PRs, tests, and design notes. For measurements, give the date or window, collection method, sample size, units, configuration, workload and missing data. Distinguish live observations, user-reported acceptance and synthetic reproduction. Keep only sanitized aggregates here.

## Diagnosis and alternatives

Separate observed facts from possible explanations. Explain the chosen mechanism, the alternatives, the tradeoffs, and the evidence that would change the conclusion.

## Implementation and review

Link the relevant code, tests and design notes. Explain what changed and how it addresses the mechanism. Preserve failed checks and independent review findings with their date or reviewed revision, the correction, and the final exact-head verdict. Approval of an older revision is historical evidence.

## Verification and outcome

What passed or failed, with what scope and limitations? What was measured, as opposed to only implemented or tested? Compare like with like. A passing health check doesn't demonstrate behavioral quality, and a smaller number doesn't establish a saving. State the remaining acceptance checks explicitly.
